## Edit checklist (every video)

Watch once **with sound off**. If you cannot tell the job from the on-screen type, rewrite the supers, do not add more talk.

Watch once **with sound on**. If the URL is not held long enough to screenshot, it failed.

Click the first-comment paths in a private window. If anything 404s, fix the comment before you boost.
