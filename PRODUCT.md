# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is the owner-publisher operating VettaJimale.Tech and related editorial projects. Approved editors and authors may later work in the same studio with narrower capabilities.

They use the CMS to publish articles and landing pages, manage reusable media and structured editorial records, connect content to audience-growth journeys, and understand what needs attention without editing code.

The public website is a separate brand surface. This document treats the authenticated CMS and visual builder as the default product surface.

## Product Purpose

Provide one dependable publishing workspace where an administrator can:

- Create, preview, publish, revise, and recover pages and editorial content.
- Build responsive pages visually while preserving controlled structure and accessible output.
- Manage shared media, settings, navigation, content relationships, and reusable sections.
- Deliver resources and follow up with eligible contacts through explicit, auditable workflows.
- See real operational status and outcomes without fabricated metrics or nonfunctional modules.

Success means routine publishing and audience workflows are understandable, reversible, secure, and complete. Draft work never leaks into live content, shared records do not drift into conflicting copies, and unfinished capabilities are identified honestly.

## Brand Personality

Practical, precise, and candid.

The studio should feel familiar to experienced WordPress and visual-builder users without copying those products. It should favor earned familiarity, direct labels, compact controls, and visible system status over decorative novelty.

## Anti-references

- A WordPress-sized navigation filled with empty or pretend modules.
- An Elementor clone or any use of proprietary assets, code, or trademarked visual identity.
- Decorative dashboard cards, fabricated analytics, or impressive-looking numbers without defined data.
- Hidden permissions that rely only on disabled controls instead of server and database enforcement.
- Freeform page-builder behavior that sacrifices responsive structure for arbitrary positioning.
- A plugin marketplace, unrestricted executable HTML, or broad automation engine before the core workflows are secure.
- Replacing stable public-site or signup behavior with an incomplete CMS equivalent.

## Design Principles

1. **The workflow is the product.** Design each feature around a complete everyday job, including validation, recovery, and a clear success state.
2. **Preserve what already works.** Extend stable publishing, builder, and funnel paths incrementally instead of rewriting them.
3. **Show the system truth.** Distinguish draft from published, configured from disconnected, exact from approximate, and complete from partial.
4. **One shared record, deliberate publication.** Reference media, settings, relationships, and reusable content by identity; publish changes explicitly.
5. **Familiar controls, strong boundaries.** Use standard product patterns while enforcing permissions, validation, responsive structure, and safe defaults beneath the interface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for public and administrative interfaces.

- Every drag-and-drop action needs a usable keyboard alternative.
- Preserve visible focus, semantic labels, validation guidance, and sufficient contrast.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid autoplay audio.
- Do not use color as the only indication of status, selection, or error.
- Keep dense controls legible and operable at browser zoom and on narrower screens.
- Require alt text and captions where the content or workflow needs them.
